# Asset descriptions

All assets are real 1920x1080 gameplay screenshots captured live from the
running game (localhost:3100) via staged demo poses. HUD shows: debug overlay
(top-left), minimap (left), 14-slot hotbar (bottom), crosshair.

- `game-vista.png` — HERO. Sunny elevated vista over a river valley: sand
  beaches, blue water, green hills, pixel trees both sides, big sky. Held
  grass block bottom-right. Clean HUD, no overlays. Safe zone: huge sky upper
  half for type.
- `game-overlay.png` — Same vista with the HYDRA L2 overlay open top-right
  (head status: Open, tx valid 4/0, snapshot #116, UTxOs 125, fuel 999,876 ₳,
  last tx hash) and the on-chain activity feed bottom-right (break/place
  records with tx hashes + snapshot numbers), plus system chat line
  bottom-left. THE on-chain proof shot.
- `game-mine.png` — First-person mining a diamond ore block: crack-stage
  overlay visible on the speckled cyan ore, hand mid-swing. Shows hold-to-mine.
- `game-tnt.png` — TNT block centered in crosshair with classic red/white TNT
  texture, second TNT on the ground, TNT held in hand bottom-right. Trees +
  green field backdrop.
- `game-cave.png` — Underground cave interior lit warm by the miner's
  headlamp; stone walls, tunnel depth. Moody, dark edges.
- `game-night.png` — Night scene under stars: zombie (green head, blue jeans,
  arms out) looming right in front of the camera, second zombie behind,
  clouds + starfield sky. Horror beat.
- `game-multi.png` — Two remote player avatars (blocky bodies, name tags
  "steve-2", "steve-3") standing on the terrain near the player, minimap with
  red dots. Multiplayer shot.
